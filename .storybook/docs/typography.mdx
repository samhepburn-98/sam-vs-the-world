# Typography

Two Barlow voices with strict jobs, and neither borrows the other's:

- **Barlow Condensed** (600/700/800) shouts in uppercase — headlines,
  straps, section labels, scores, the brand lozenge. It renders through the
  `font-heading` utility and, for titles, only ever through the three
  primitives below.
- **Barlow** (400/500/600) speaks in sentences — prose, receipts, table
  numbers. It is the default `font-sans` on `<html>`, so body copy needs no
  font class at all.

## The heading primitives

Every title in the app renders through `PageTitle`, `SectionTitle`, or
`Overline` (`src/components/typography.tsx`) — the sizes cannot drift
between pages because there is exactly one place they are defined.

## Rules of the voice

- **Scores and stat values** are Barlow Condensed extrabold with
  `tabular-nums`, so columns of numbers align.
- **Uppercase belongs to the display face only.** Barlow is never
  uppercased; condensed headings always are.
- **The skew is a badge treatment, not a text style**: `skew-badge` leans
  the lozenge, `skew-badge-text` counter-skews the text upright. Only
  brand marks and the VS get it.
- **The muted tone** (`--muted-foreground`) is the floor for text that must
  be read; anything dimmer is decoration.
