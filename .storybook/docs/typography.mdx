# Typography

Two voices with strict jobs (rule 06), and neither borrows the other's:

- **Chakra Petch** shouts in uppercase — headings, labels, scores. It renders
  through the `font-heading` utility and, for titles, only ever through the
  three primitives below.
- **Nunito Sans** speaks in sentences — prose, receipts, table numbers. It is
  the default `font-sans` on `<html>`, so body copy needs no font class at all.

## The heading primitives

Every title in the app renders through `PageTitle`, `SectionTitle`, or
`Overline` (`src/components/typography.tsx`) — the sizes cannot drift between
pages because there is exactly one place they are defined.

<div style={{ display: "flex", flexDirection: "column", gap: 24, background: "var(--background)", border: "1px solid var(--border)", borderRadius: 12, padding: 24, margin: "16px 0 32px" }}>

      Body copy in Nunito Sans. Receipts, explanations, and table numbers all
      speak in this voice — sentence case, no tracking, no shouting.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## Rules of the voice

- **Scores and stat values** are Chakra Petch with `tabular-nums`, so columns
  of numbers align.
- **Uppercase belongs to the display face only.** Nunito Sans is never
  uppercased; Chakra Petch headings always are.
- **The muted label tone** (`--muted-foreground`) is the floor for text that
  must be read (rule 08); anything dimmer is decoration.
- **Weights**: Chakra Petch ships at 500 / 600 / 700 — no light weights, the
  display voice doesn't whisper. Nunito Sans is variable.
