# The Broadcast rules

What keeps Broadcast a design system instead of a costume. Every rule
exists to stop the same failure: decoration leaking into data.

<Unstyled>
  <div
    className="text-foreground"
    style={{
      display: "flex",
      flexDirection: "column",
      gap: 10,
      margin: "16px 0 32px",
      fontFamily: "var(--font-sans)",
    }}
  >
    <Rule n="01" title="The card is the one crafted object.">
      The FUT player card keeps its texture and shine; everything around it is a
      flat graphic. Nothing else gets texture, gloss, or depth effects.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="02" title="Flat, always.">
      No gradients exist — not on surfaces, bars, charts, or text. Depth comes
      from the three surface steps (deep, floor, panel) and the accent bars.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="03" title="Square cut.">
      Broadcast graphics are cut, not rounded: --radius is zero and stays zero.
      Only dots (serving marker, ball dots, avatars) are circles.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="04" title="The colour law.">
      Every value that belongs to a player wears their side — ember for player
      one, blue for player two — scores, bars, tags, chart lines, no exceptions.
      Fills use the base tones; text on dark uses the strong tones.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="05" title="Results speak status.">
      W/L/D chips, trends, and cautions use the semantic status tokens, never
      the team colours — a loss is red whichever side lost it.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="06" title="Two voices, strict jobs.">
      Barlow Condensed shouts in uppercase: headlines, straps, labels, scores.
      Barlow speaks in sentences: prose, receipts, table numbers. Neither
      borrows the other's job.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="07" title="The skew is earned by badges.">
      The -10° lean belongs to the brand lozenge and the VS mark. Panels, chips,
      and text stay upright — a screen of leaning boxes is a carnival, not a
      broadcast.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="08" title="Receipts survive the redesign.">
      Every rate shows its denominator; under-sampled values show a quiet dash,
      never an invented number. Graphics dress the truth, they do not replace
      it.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="09" title="Motion is compositor-only and rare.">
      Transform and opacity, 300ms ease-out, on hero moments. No animated
      filters, no layout animation — the Safari lesson is law.
    </Rule>
    <Rule n="10" title="Phone-first, thumb-first.">
      Every surface designs at 390px first; hit targets are 44px minimum; the
      logger works one-handed mid-game. Desktop is the phone layout given room,
      not a different app.
    </Rule>
  </div>
</Unstyled>

**Migration note**: Broadcast replaced Ultimate (decision 17): the
gradients, gold accent, spotlight staging, and card-glow sprites retired;
`--primary-strong` and `--panel-deep` joined the tokens; Barlow Condensed
and Barlow replaced Chakra Petch and Nunito Sans. Typography lands via the
existing `PageTitle` / `SectionTitle` / `Overline` primitives — same seams,
new voice.
