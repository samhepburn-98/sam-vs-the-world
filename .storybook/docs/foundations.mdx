# Sam vs the World — design system

This Storybook is the living reference for the app's **Broadcast** design
system: sports-TV information graphics built around the FUT-style player
cards. Every reusable component gets a story here; the pages of the app are
compositions of what you see in the sidebar.

Everything renders from the **live CSS variables** in `src/styles.css`, so
these docs cannot drift from the app.

## The one-paragraph version

Broadcast is **dark-only studio graphics** — flat graphite-navy surfaces in
three steps, square corners everywhere, Barlow Condensed shouting the
headlines from skewed lozenge badges. The two team colours are the only
accents, and **every value that belongs to a player wears their side**:
ember for player one, blue for player two. Results speak through the status
tokens as W/L/D chips. The player card is the one crafted, textured object;
everything around it is a flat graphic with a slot for a number.

The details live in the neighbouring pages:

- **Colors** — the palette, the colour law, status tokens, and the chart
  ramp. No gradients exist.
- **Typography** — the two Barlow voices and the heading primitives.
- **The Broadcast rules** — what keeps this a design system instead of a
  costume.

## The graphic shapes

The system is a kit of broadcast graphics, each a story in the sidebar:
**Ticker** (full-bleed strip), **Callout** (accent-bar pundit panel),
**ScoreStrip** (deep full-time graphic), **ResultChip / FormGuide** (W/L/D
in status colours), **StatRow / StatTile** (label-and-number lower-thirds),
and the skewed **brand lozenge** (`skew-badge` utilities).

## Where things go

- `src/components/ui` — shadcn primitives, themed by tokens only.
- `src/components` — shared composed components (typography, the broadcast
  graphics, court, rally).
- `src/features/*` — feature-specific components; a feature never imports a
  sibling feature.
- Stories live next to their component as `*.stories.tsx`.

## Working rules

- **Semantic over raw.** Components reference tokens (`text-success`,
  `bg-p2`, `text-primary-strong`), never one-off colours.
- **Headings are componentised.** `PageTitle`, `SectionTitle`, and
  `Overline` are the only way titles render.
- **Charts are recharts** wherever a chart type exists for the data; court
  visuals stay hand-drawn SVG.
- **Phone-first.** Every surface works at 390px with 44px hit targets before
  it earns a desktop layout.
