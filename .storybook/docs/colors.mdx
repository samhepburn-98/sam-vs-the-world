# Colors

Every colour below renders from the **live CSS variables** in
`src/styles.css`. Broadcast is dark-only — a studio has one lighting rig —
and **flat: no gradient exists anywhere in the system**. Depth comes from
the three surface steps and the accent bars.

## The colour law

The two team colours are the only accents. **Every value that belongs to a
player wears their side** — scores, bars, trait tags, chart lines — with no
exceptions. Each side has a fill tone (for surfaces, with dark text on
ember) and a strong tone (for text on dark surfaces).

## Status tokens

Jira-style semantic tokens, lifted to read on dark. Results speak through
these — the W/L/D chips, trends, callout states — never through the team
colours.
